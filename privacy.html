Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you visit yourpricing.pro or contact me, as required by Article 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

Pavel Storozhuk
Frankfurt am Main, Germany (postal address available on request by email)
info@yourpricing.pro

At a glance

  • This site sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device.
  • It uses no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Its fonts and images come from this site, and it embeds no social media or other third-party content.
  • Personal data is processed only to deliver the site securely and to handle messages you send me.

Hosting and delivery

When you open this site, your browser's requests pass through two providers, which necessarily receive your IP address along with the date and time, the page requested and technical details of your browser:

  • Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, delivers the site and protects it from attacks (privacy policy).
  • GitHub, Inc., San Francisco, USA, hosts the site through GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes (privacy statement).

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: my legitimate interest in delivering the site securely and reliably. These providers set their own log retention periods, as described in their policies.

Contact form

The contact form sends your name, email address and message to Web3Forms, a service operated by Web3Creative from India (support@web3forms.com; privacy policy, data processing agreement), which forwards them to my mailbox. Web3Forms also processes technical data such as your IP address, the time and the referring page.

Web3Forms uses Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare and Hetzner as infrastructure providers. To filter spam, it sends your IP address and email address to CleanTalk Inc. (privacy policy) and Automattic Inc. for its Akismet service (privacy policy). Web3Forms keeps submissions for up to three years unless they are deleted earlier.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message concerns a possible engagement, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: my legitimate interest in answering messages and keeping out spam. Using the form is voluntary. You can email me directly instead.

Email

My mailbox, including messages from the contact form, is provided by Apple Distribution International Limited, Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland, through iCloud Mail (privacy policy). Apple says personal data is generally stored by Apple Inc. in the United States. The legal basis is the same as for the contact form.

I delete messages once the matter is closed, unless they must be kept by law. Messages I must keep under German tax law, such as those relating to an invoice, are kept for the legal period, generally six to eight years.

LinkedIn

This site links to my LinkedIn profile. It is a plain link, so LinkedIn receives nothing from this site unless you follow it. After that, LinkedIn's privacy policy applies.

Transfers outside the EU

  • Cloudflare and GitHub are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (participant list).
  • Web3Forms operates from India, for which there is no adequacy decision. Transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, included in its data processing agreement, which also covers its sub-processors.
  • Apple relies on Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask Apple for a copy.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • have it corrected (Art. 16) or erased (Art. 17);
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18);
  • receive it in a portable format (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f), on grounds relating to your situation (Art. 21).

To exercise these rights, email me at the address above. You can also complain to a data protection authority. Mine is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (datenschutz.hessen.de).

I make no automated decisions about you, and no profiling takes place.

Changes

I update this policy when the site or its providers change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
